SECTION – 6A SCOPE OF WORK










	•	Scope of the Project
	Under this Agreement, the scope of the Project (the “Scope of the Project”) shall mean and include: Project Site location, Development of the Project and Facilities, Specifications and Standards.
	•	Project site location is as referred in Clause 1.2 of Volume: 1 Section 1: Project information 
	•	Development of the Project and Facilities: 

	•	Construction of North Breakwater (111m) and South Breakwater (121m) 
	•	Construction of Marine Structures which includes Precast Retaining wall – length of 114.0m, RCC Piled Berthing jetty – length of 100.0m, RC sloping hard & Beach landing – length of 100m, other Onshore facilities and allied works etc.
	•	Dredging and Reclamation– Dredging the basin including dredge disposal and reclamation by backfilling for development of the yard to construct Onshore facilities
	•	The scope of work of the Project outlined briefly herein is only indicative and detailed scope of work will be as specified in the Specifications and Drawings given elsewhere in this bid document. The Contractor, being an EPC Contractor, is however entirely responsible for proper design of works, procurement and Construction of project structures complying with the Authority’s requirement.
	•	The scope of Tender shall cover all the works listed below but not limited to following for completion of the project and its performance:
	•	Carryout Engineering and Design of the North & South Breakwaters, Dredging & Reclamation, Marine structures which includes Precast Retaining wall – length of 114.0m, RCC Piled Berthing jetty – length of 100.0m, RC sloping hard & Beach landing – length of 100m, other Onshore facilities etc. including Navigational aids as per design criteria given in the tender.
	•	With regard to Procurement of stones for Construction of Breakwater, the Contractor is advised to procure the stones from nearby sources as per specifications and standards. 
	•	Provide and mobilize all Plant and Equipment required 
	•	Construction of the above works 
	•	Carryout temporary protection to Breakwaters during monsoon and temporary works for the marine structures.
	•	Any additional works required to be carried out to mitigate adverse effects on the environment and also as directed by the Authority without any cost incurred to the Employer.
	•	Any incidental and ancillary works that may be required to complete the main works.
	•	The Period for completion of the Project works including Construction of break water, marine structure with allied civil works, dredging and reclamation for (Name of work), on EPC basis is XX months from the date of LoA (“Construction Period”).
	•	Performance and fulfilment of all other obligations of the Contractor in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement and matters incidental thereto or necessary for the performance of any or all of the obligations of the Contractor under this Agreement.
The Employer’s Requirements are that the Contractor shall carryout the Engineering, Design, Procurement of materials and Construction / installation of all the items listed in this tender document as per the scope, design criteria, specifications and drawings outlined in this tender document. For this purpose, the Tenderer shall conduct all necessary field tests and surveys to satisfy / verify himself regarding the correctness of the data furnished vis-à-vis actual condition. The tenderer may also carry out such surveys and investigations even before the submission of tender. No claim whatsoever will be entertained for any variation between the actual site condition met with during the execution of the work and those indicated herein. The Contractor shall be deemed to have examined the site and familiarized himself with all existing site conditions. He shall accept the Site in the existing condition at the time of award of the Contract.
The specifications provided in the tender together with enclosed scope drawings outlines the functional requirements and the operating characteristics which the structures must fulfil. Alternative technical features/designs other than those specified may be acceptable subject to the approval of the Employer. In any case, the performance of the system/ equipment, if any, delivered shall be guaranteed in every detail by the Contractor. Overall dimensions (boundary dimensions) and functional requirements as specified shall be strictly adhered to. The scope of work outlined below is for overall understanding of the Works and does not absolve the Contractor from successful commissioning and operation of the Works with best available latest technology. Any item/equipment not listed but required for completion of the Works shall be considered as included in the scope of the Contractor.
